# Diagram

**Draft, 2026-10-29: for agreement.** A diagram is the view you enter from a chip: one block's inside, drawn alone, its cards placed automatically and joined by lines. The map summarises and the diagram explains.


## Purpose

| Question | How the diagram answers |
|---|---|
| *how do the parts fit together?* | cards placed by layout: layered where lines join, packed where none do, banded by region |
| *what flows where?* | lines with direction, drawn once at the level where they cross, several between a pair counted |
| *what kind of picture is this?* | its view, picked by the block's kind: internal, allocation, sequence, class, entity, registry, treemap |


## Information

| Layer of the picture | Shows |
|---|---|
| **cards** | each part, by card.md |
| **lines** | relations, by line.md |
| **holders** | groups, regions, grids: the structure that shapes the cards |
| **notation** | notes, labels and marks, kept quiet |
| **frame** | crumbs at the top, over a head band in the diagram kind's colour: its icon, name and kind. An empty layer says why it is empty |


## Differs from the map

| | map | diagram |
|---|---|---|
| shows | many elements, summarised | one block's inside, in full |
| layout | treemap: area is amount | by kind: area is the card's content |
| lines | none | drawn |
| edits | never | always |
| colour edge | a diagram chip wears its kind's hue | each card wears its own colour |
