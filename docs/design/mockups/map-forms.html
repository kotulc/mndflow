Map forms

Three questions from docs/design, drawn on the shop's models map: should a leaf on the map be a card, should a box's title carry counts, and should explorer rows carry counts. One zoom drives every map on the page, so you can also watch levels come and go. Hover any chip, card or box title for its hover card.

box counts

1 · Leaves: chip or card

A leaf is a part that gathers nothing: Customer, Gateway, the runbook. Today it is a chip like everything else. Proposed: it is a card at its small face, so the map reads chips gather, cards are parts. Inside a chip, the tiles are cards at their smallest either way.

today · leaves are chips
proposed · leaves are cards
  • Chip: flat neutral fill, colour edge on the left, previews what it gathers as tiles.
  • Card: bordered all round and tinted with its own colour, seats on its walls, previews its own face (attributes, body, description).
  • Zoom: layout never moves. Text holds a readable size (it scales with zoom only between 85% and 115%), so zooming in leaves room for more rows and zooming out drops them: preview, then facts, then the name. A level drops only 15% below where it was added.

2 · Box titles with counts

With box counts on, a box's title says what it holds after its name, in the facts strip's words: blocks, lines, diagrams. The counts drop first when the title runs out of room, and the title goes when the box's top band is too thin to hold it. Switch them off in the bar to compare. Both maps above follow the switch.

3 · Explorer rows: colour edge, with or without counts

Every row now wears its card's colour edge (decided). The open question is a quiet count, right-aligned, on rows the map draws as chips: a definition's uses, a diagram's blocks. Folders and packages carry no count, as boxes on the map carry none of their own in the row.
