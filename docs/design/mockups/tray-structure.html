Tray structure

One tray, the same on every selection. One tab bar for every element: overview · card · settings · components · usages · contents. A tab that does not apply is drawn faint, never hidden. Every tab has one layout: the card in the left column with that tab's filters under it, and the list they narrow on the right. Filters are explorer rows local to the tab: a mark, a name and a count, single-select, with an all row first. Pick an element and a tab, click a filter, hover a filter to light its members on the card, and try the three widths. The overview shows a map of where it lives. Questions 1 to 5 are decided; each is drawn both ways, and the verdict records them.

Where it lives

Under the card, the overview shows where the element sits in its whole model or package: quiet nested boxes sized by how many blocks each holds, the top level named, the path down to the element drawn firmer and the element itself lit. Nothing in it repeats a chip; it only says where. A line lights both its ends. On the overview only: other tabs keep their filter rows under the card. Its path heads the answers column.
