Tray lists

v9. Components shows the card left and the lists right; a chip appears only where it is the list drawn (a definition's uses or records). Every list opens on a ruled band and every row has a rule under it, one table look; sections carry no count, their bands do. A definition is told apart without dashes: its card is an outline with no ground, and a filled definition badge in its colour sits in the tray head and on the card; a use is filled, with a quiet use badge. Section names are stronger; band labels sit flush left. Components and usages rebuilt from Overview Forms for a tray whose first job is to say what an element is. The left is the visual; the right is every section it has, titled, each with an i saying what it is, its rows on shared columns. Nothing to add, no groups to pick, no gaps for what it lacks. Hover a row to light it on the card. The head says plainly whether it is a definition or a use. Drag tray width to watch it reflow.

Three card classes, one role

partthe default: any card that holds no layer. Its face lists its members: attributes, then behaviours as signatures, its ports on its walls. A class, a record, a registry are all parts
diagrama card that holds a layer: its face is its chip. Its tab is contents
documenttext and no members: its face is its text
definition, a roleany of them can be a definition: an outline with no ground, a filled badge in its colour, and a uses tab

What changes

read first
components shows every section the element has, at once, as the overview does. No picker, no add lists, no rows for what it lacks: an empty section is simply absent, and the facts strip says what is there
class face
a part with behaviours draws as a class card: attributes, then its behaviours by signature in the last section. A behaviour with steps says so and opens its flow
role
a definition wears a filled badge in its colour, in the tray head and on its card, and its card is an outline with no ground; a use is filled and its badge is quiet
usages
the definition's chip of uses on the left; uses by place on the right. A record definition's uses are its records, so they read as a table under each place
edits
basic edits (a name, a description, a style or setting) belong to the card and settings tabs, still to be rethought; components and usages only read
