What changes, across every case

tray head
the identity row (colour edge, name, kind, marks) and the facts strip, replacing today's tray header, the overview's caption and the counts at the bottom. A definition wears the Def mark instead of a definition tag; a line wears its swatch
sections
card → picture → why it matters → how it is used → where it lives → where it sits, each titled, for every element, lines included
width
one column under 560 px, two (card and picture | answers) to 960 px, three (card | picture | answers) above
face
the large face, as a diagram draws it, above the picture. A blank block shows its card; attributes it does not answer show as dashed rows
picture
what it gathers (its chip), else its place (the room's chip, its tile lit). Eight special pictures become two sources; connections move to how it is used. Never an empty canvas, never a scaled drawing
answers
its own only: a description borrowed from its definition is gone, and its absence is a gap. Counts move to the facts strip
legibility
tiles never drop below their name; what does not fit folds into +n more. The picture is the map's chip, so it scales as the map does
unchanged
the kinds an empty block may become, order in its room with earlier and later, open and read
